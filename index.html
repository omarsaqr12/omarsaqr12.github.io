02 / Research

What can a model
actually learn?

Wireless localization, federated unlearning,
and grounded language and multimodal systems.

I study how models learn from incomplete signals, transfer knowledge across modalities, and respond when a training contribution is removed. Across localization and language tasks, I use controlled comparisons to understand both the gains and the limits.

Indoor localizationCross-modal learningFederated unlearningLanguage & grounding
2026ACM SIGSPATIAL

Accepted paper · Equal contributor

Loc-Erase: Spatially-Aware Federated Unlearning for Wi-Fi-Based Indoor Localization

Mostafa Gaafar*, Omar Saqr*, Hamada Rizk, Moustafa Youssef

Studies approximate client removal on UJIIndoorLoc, comparing calibration-based unlearning with retraining. Spatial-zone evaluation shows how exclusive coverage affects removal cost and exposes residual influence.

When Cross-Modal Distillation Can’t Help: A Recoverability Diagnosis of WiFi RTT to RSS Positioning

Omar Saqr*, Mostafa Gaafar*, Hamada Rizk, Moustafa Youssef

Tests the limits of RTT-to-RSS distillation with matched comparisons and teacher-destruction controls, separating apparent transfer from augmentation effects and examining the uncertainty of output-KD gains.
