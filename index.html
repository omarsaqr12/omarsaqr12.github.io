02 / Research

What can a model
actually learn?

My work examines efficient learning,
cross-modal transfer, and reliable evaluation.

Indoor localization brings a practical constraint: the best training signal may be unavailable at inference. I study how knowledge distillation helps—and where the information available to a student sets the limit.

Cross-modal learningIndoor localizationEfficient sequence models
